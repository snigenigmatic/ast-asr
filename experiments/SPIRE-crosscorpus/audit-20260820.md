## Two facts that constrain the analysis

1. **Only 13 of the 17 shipped languages appear in the validation split.** Dogri,
   Gujarati, Maithili, and Sindhi contribute zero validation utterances. They are
   train-split-only and are therefore absent from every reported number here.
2. **The per-language tail is severe.** Kashmiri has 2 utterances, Nepali 34,
   Punjabi 50, Konkani 78. This is exactly why the protocol pools to family and
   treats per-language cells as descriptive with no hypothesis test. Reporting a
   2-utterance "Kashmiri WER" would be meaningless.

Neither fact threatens the family-level or speaker-level analysis, and both are
disclosed rather than smoothed over.
