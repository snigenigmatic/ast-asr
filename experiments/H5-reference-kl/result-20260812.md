# H5 fixed-reference KL result — 2026-08-12

**Classification:** exploratory, one-seed, fold-0 demographic-profile-cluster engineering result; `publication_valid: false`.

## Decision

The H5 **mechanism** result is supported: adding the fixed-SFT sampled-K3 penalty (`beta=0.04`) retained genuine movement and all safety checks over 40 cycles while reducing peak sampled K3 from `0.02491910` to `0.01642897`.

The H5 **efficacy** result is unconfirmed. The one-seed point estimate meets the registered primary point gate: treatment minus control worst family-condition WER is `-0.02217742` (a 2.2177 absolute-point reduction), while clean overall WER changes by only `+0.00066635`. But the 10,000-resample paired profile-cluster bootstrap interval for the primary delta is `[-0.02217740, +0.05238690]`; it includes harm. The resampling units are 39 fold-0 demographic-profile clusters, **not authoritative speakers**, so this does not repair the unresolved identity limitation.

Consequently, H5 does not automatically unlock a three-seed run, five-fold training, learning-rate selection, or a publication claim. A separately reviewed and committed replication protocol is required before more seeds. The robustness evidence is mixed: the treatment improves the worst cell and worst-20%-cluster summary, but both aggregate noise conditions and noise amplification are worse than the matched control.
