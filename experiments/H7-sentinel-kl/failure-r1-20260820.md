## Diagnosis

The input lock froze the bytes of a waveform generated by
`torch.randn` plus floating-point SNR mixing. The same committed lock and first
source WAV reproduce the clean hash, SNR, and expected noisy hash exactly under
the local lock-building environment (`torch 2.13.0+cpu`, Windows 11). The
authorized Modal image used `torch 2.11.0+cu128` on Linux. Modal reproduced the
seed and SNR but not the noisy waveform bytes.

This demonstrates that the lock's bitwise noisy-waveform contract was
environment-coupled across these two runtimes. PyTorch explicitly does not
guarantee completely reproducible or
bitwise-identical results across releases and platforms:
<https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/notes/randomness> and
<https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/main/notes/numerical_accuracy.html>.

The evidence isolates the fault to the random-normal sample and/or subsequent
floating-point noise mixing; it cannot separate those two operations because
the failed run did not persist the produced noise tensor. It does rule out
source-audio bytes, row identity, seed, and SNR selection.
