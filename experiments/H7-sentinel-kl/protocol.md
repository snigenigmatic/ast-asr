## Phase B — fixed-policy bank rescore

Only after Phase A passes, reuse its validated production-shape cycle-027 rows
and score each of the remaining 27 banks exactly once under the same frozen
policy/reference. Phase A's explicit determinism repeat is the only duplicated
model score. For every valid output token, preserve and emit `token_id`, policy
FP32 log probability, reference FP32 log probability, log ratio, K3
contribution, mask, bank/cycle, candidate, utterance, family, condition, and
within-candidate token position. Derive masked, token-weighted summaries at:

| Level | Required fields |
| --- | --- |
| token | K3 contribution, token position, valid mask, candidate/utterance/group identity |
| candidate | masked K3, valid-token count, candidate index |
| utterance | masked K3, total valid tokens, family, condition |
| family × condition | masked K3 and total valid tokens |
| global bank | masked K3/token, total valid tokens, and `<0.1` / `>=0.1` classification |
| length | candidate valid-token length plus predeclared descriptive K3-versus-length summaries |

No aggregation may silently include padding tokens, replace an empty/invalid
denominator, or change weighting from the explicit valid-token denominator.
Family × condition and global figures are descriptive decompositions of the
saved banks, not causal attributions.

For every valid token, define
`d = log_pi_SFT.detach().float() - log_pi_fixed.float()` and
`k3 = expm1(d) - d`. Reject any non-finite selected log probability or K3 term,
or any selected `abs(d) > 20`; do not clamp it. Candidate, utterance, group,
and bank summaries are the sum of selected token K3 terms divided by the
corresponding selected-token count.

Any identity, determinism, finiteness, denominator, or `abs(d) > 20` stop is
classified `measurement_failed` / `non_evaluable`. It is never counted as a
bank below or above the threshold.

## Registered interpretation and adaptive-KL gate

The decision rule is only the bank classification under one fixed policy:

- If at least one saved bank is `<0.1` and at least one is `>=0.1`, threshold
  classification is bank-dependent for this fixed policy.  A controller that
  reacts to a single fresh-bank K3 crossing is then not interpretable as
  responding uniquely to policy drift; a fixed sentinel bank or an explicitly
  uncertainty-aware measurement validation is required before adaptive-KL
  control can be interpreted.
- If all 28 banks lie on one side, H7 finds no evidence of threshold flips for
  this fixed policy and these saved banks.  That limited stability result is a
  prerequisite for interpreting a single-bank controller signal, not evidence
  that adaptive KL control is safe, effective, or warranted.

Neither outcome estimates the crossing probability for fresh on-policy banks;
that would require a separately registered final-policy sampling study.

Any correlations with length, candidate/utterance composition, family, or
condition are descriptive only.  They neither identify a cause of K3 nor
authorize changing beta, a controller, or a subsequent training run.
