# Svarah: Evaluating English ASR Systems on Indian Accents

- **Authors/year:** Tahir Javed, Sakshi Joshi, Vignesh Nagarajan, Sai Sundaresan, Janki Nawale, Abhigyan Raman, Kaushal Bhogale, Pratyush Kumar, Mitesh M. Khapra (2023).

- **Verified claim:** Svarah contains 9.6 hours of transcribed Indian-English audio from **117 speakers** across 65 geographic locations, with read and spontaneous speech. The paper evaluates six open-source and two commercial ASR systems.
