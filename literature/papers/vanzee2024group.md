# Group Fairness in Multilingual Speech Recognition Models

- **Authors/year:** Anna Katrine van Zee, Marc van Zee, Anders Søgaard (2024).
- **Stable record:** [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-naacl.143/); [DOI:10.18653/v1/2024.findings-naacl.143](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2024.findings-naacl.143).
- **Verification/BibTeX:** official ACL record plus Crossref DOI record; BibTeX fetched from DOI content negotiation (also available from ACL export).
- **Verified claim:** evaluates Whisper and MMS across VoxPopuli and Common Voice with intersectional analysis. It reports that model size correlates logarithmically with worst-case performance disparities and identifies gender gaps for adolescents.
- **Method/results and reported metrics/datasets:** multilingual ASR audit across VoxPopuli/Common Voice languages and demographic groups, reporting group performance disparities rather than an intervention result.
- **Relevance:** reinforces that a better/larger ASR model need not reduce worst-group disparity.
- **Copy / do not copy:** copy worst-case and intersectional reporting. Do **not** infer that Svarah family labels are demographic identities or that larger Whisper variants are a fairness baseline.
